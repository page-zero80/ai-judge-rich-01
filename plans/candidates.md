# Candidate plans

Three different edges. Each plan body is under 1,000 characters.

## Candidate A — Year-end on-chain books for Solana DAOs (paid niche)

Product: fixed-scope year-end treasury report for one DAO: multisig token balances priced in USD, categorised flows, realised/unrealised P&L, accountant-ready CSV, from public Solana RPC + CoinGecko, delivered in 24h.
Buyer: a signer of a $50k-$50M DAO with no finance hire, reporting to tokenholders and handing 2026 books to a bookkeeper. Accountants charge $300+; we charge $150.
Channel: free sample from each DAO's public Realms/Squads treasury, one personalised note to a public signer, sample posted in its governance forum, then 60 DAOs with unreconciled proposals.
Day 1: sample MonkeDAO, Metaplex DAO, Jupiter DAO; message one signer each.
Payment: upfront USDC; no KYC or bank.
Edge: finished deliverable, not a dashboard, at half the human price; templates compound.
Worst case: $100 (free tools, paid before work).
Day 30: $100 + 4 x $150 = $700 from 60 approaches at 7% conversion.

## Candidate B — Liquidation watchdog for leveraged DAO treasuries (information asymmetry)

Product: monitor public Solana lending positions (Kamino, Solend, MarginFi) held by DAO multisigs and alert signers when the health factor approaches liquidation, with the exact repay or collateral action needed.
Buyer: DAO treasury signers who borrowed against treasury tokens and cannot watch positions 24/7; a liquidation is permanent, an alert is cheap.
Channel: scan public on-chain positions to find leveraged DAOs, identify signers from public multisig/governance pages, send a free sample alert for their own position.
Day 1: build the RPC scanner, identify five leveraged DAOs, deliver one free alert.
Price: $99/month USDC, upfront.
Edge: prevention rather than reporting; the buyer list comes from public risk data.
Worst case: $100; no capital at risk.
Day 30: fewer buyers than A, roughly $100-$400 expected.

## Candidate C — Solana NFT / .sol domain spread arbitrage (marketplace arbitrage)

Product: buy assets listed well below a defensible reference value and relist: .sol domains under recent comparable sales, NFTs under a trait-adjusted floor. Execute only when the spread exceeds fees plus a 10% safety margin.
Buyer: none; the agent is principal and earns the spread.
Channel: public Magic Eden/Tensor APIs and SNS sale history; automated scan and 48h dry-run first.
Day 1: build the scanner, log apparent gaps, verify reference prices against completed sales before spending.
Price: the spread, typically 5-15% per flip after fees.
Edge: trait and comparable-sale pricing rather than naive floor sniping; hard rule against illiquid assets.
Worst case: $95-$100 if a purchase cannot be resold; capital is at risk, so not strictly non-negative.
Day 30: highly variable, maybe +$20-$100; professional bots make the edge thin.
